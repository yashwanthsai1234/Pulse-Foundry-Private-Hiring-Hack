## Decisions with evidence
- Schedule date range for COV-RN-DAILY: OFF cells are not ShiftRecords, so a facility range from shifts gives 6 days for Riverdale (CNA Mon-Sat) and misses Sun 09-20 for Bayside. expected.json wants 7 and 09-20, so the range is widened to Monday..Sunday weeks. Contract gap: silver should expose the schedule date span.
- LIC-WORKED-EXPIRED skips a shift when the person holds another in-scope credential valid that day (expired CNA plus valid RN is not a violation).
- LIC-NONE-FOR-ROLE (no credential at all) and LIC-SCOPE (credentials exist, none in scope) are split so one fact is not reported twice.
- SHIFT-OVERLAP is SQL (interval self-join), not Python: 8 lines.
- `Claim.observed_at` is the build time (records carry no file timestamp), so same-source ties are broken by claim_id.
